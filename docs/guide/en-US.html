1Only on first launch

First-run setup

Before you ever see a chart, the app asks two quick questions. This only happens once — from then on it opens straight to the Summary screen.

1Starting balance

LET'S GET STARTED!

Enter what you currently own — your net worth. This becomes the baseline every later balance is calculated from: as spending and earnings come in, the app adds and subtracts from this number rather than starting at zero.

2Starting categories

STARTING CATEGORIES

Pick which of the built-in categories (Food, Transportation, Leisure, Health, Education…) you actually want, or type your own and add it to the list. Everything is pre-selected — tap a chip to drop it. You can still add or remove categories later from the entry form.

2Also first run only

Importing your spreadsheet

The screen titled “Where Did My Money Go?” is where you hand the app your existing records — a bank export, a budget spreadsheet, whatever you've been tracking in .csv or .ods.

📄Three ways to start

  • SELECT CSV/ODS FILE — pick a single .csv/.ods file.
  • SELECT FOLDER — pick a whole folder; every valid spreadsheet inside gets imported at once.
  • CONTINUE WITHOUT IMPORTING — skip straight to an empty app and add entries by hand instead.

✓What counts as valid

Each file needs two tables side by side on one sheet: a spending table and an earnings table. Note that the app looks for the column headers written in Portuguese — Data, Valor, Descrição, Categoria — regardless of the app's display language, since that's how it recognizes the two tables. After picking a file or folder, the app shows a report — files that matched are listed under FILES LOADED, anything rejected shows up with the reason why (wrong format, empty file, missing columns). Rejected files are simply skipped; they don't block the valid ones.

Re-importing is safe. Each row is de-duplicated by date, amount, description, category and type, so importing the same file twice — or a folder you've already imported once — won't create duplicate entries.
Home · Summary tab

The Summary screen

This is the app's home base — a dashboard of where you stand, for whatever period you're looking at.

💰Total assets

TOTAL PATRIMONY

Your starting balance from onboarding. Tap the number to edit it directly, any time.

⚖️Balance after spending

BALANCE AFTER SPENDING

Assets + earnings − spending for the selected period. Cyan when positive, red when you're in the hole.

📅Period selector

A dropdown (Day / Week / Month / Year / Custom) that filters every chart and total on the screen. Choosing Custom reveals a date-range picker so you can look at, say, a specific trip or billing cycle.

📈Charts, split by type

Two tabs — SPENDING and EARNINGS — switch what the charts below them show:

  • A line chart of amounts over time for the selected period.
  • A pie chart breaking the same period down by category.

ΣTotals

Two bold cards, TOTAL SPENDING and TOTAL EARNINGS, showing the sum for whatever period and category filter are currently active — the same numbers the charts above are visualizing.

⋮Filtering by category or date

The ⋮ icon in the top bar opens FILTER, a dialog to narrow everything on the Summary screen down to one category and/or a custom date range — useful for answering “how much did I spend on Leisure this year?” without leaving the dashboard.

Home · Entries tab

The Entries list

Switch to ENTRIES in the bottom navigation to leave the charts behind and see every individual transaction.

📋A scrollable ledger

Entries load a page at a time as you scroll, newest first. Each row shows the date, description, a pink or cyan tag for expense vs. income, the category, and the amount in your chosen currency. A jump-to-top button appears once you've scrolled a way down.

The + button

Adding entries

The pink + button, floating on the Summary tab, opens a menu with three ways to add data after the initial import.

＋Add manually

Opens ADD ENTRY: date, amount, description, a category dropdown (with an inline “add new category” option), and an Expense/Income toggle. One tap of SAVE and it's in the ledger.

📄Import a file

The same single-file picker from onboarding — for adding a new statement later on.

🗂️Import a folder

The same folder picker — bulk-import a batch of new files at once.

The ⚙ icon

Settings

Reached via the gear icon on the Summary screen's top bar.

💱Currency

Every amount in the app — cards, charts, the ledger — is formatted using whatever currency you pick here: BRL (R$), USD ($), EUR (€), GBP (£) or ARS ($).

⚠️Danger zone

DANGER ZONE

Irreversible. This wipes every imported and manually-added entry plus your custom categories, resetting the app to its empty state. There's a confirmation dialog, and no undo after that — it's a full local reset, not an archive.
